# 🧠 My Common English Mistakes

Use these mistakes as my personal English training priorities.

## 1. Verb Tenses ⭐⭐⭐

I often confuse verb tenses when speaking.

Focus especially on:

* Present Simple vs Present Continuous
* Past Simple vs Past Continuous
* Past Simple vs Present Perfect
* Present Perfect vs Present Perfect Continuous
* Past Perfect
* Future forms
* Future Perfect

Examples:

❌ I live here for five years.
✅ I have lived here for five years.

❌ They were eating so much they went to sleep.
✅ They had eaten so much they went to sleep.

---

## 2. Conditionals ⭐⭐⭐

I sometimes use the wrong tense in conditional sentences.

Practice:

* First Conditional
* Second Conditional
* Third Conditional

Especially:

❌ If I have time, I would call you.
✅ If I have time, I will call you.

❌ If you will ask her...
✅ If you ask her...

❌ If I were you, I will go...
✅ If I were you, I would go...

---

## 3. Gerund vs Infinitive ⭐⭐⭐

This is one of my main weaknesses.

I often use **to + verb** when I need **verb + -ing**, or vice versa.

Practice:

* enjoy + -ing
* would mind + -ing
* what about + -ing
* want + to + verb
* would like + to + verb
* decide + to + verb
* prefer + to + verb

Examples:

❌ Would you mind to give me...?
✅ Would you mind giving me...?

❌ What about we go...?
✅ What about going...?

❌ I would like talking...
✅ I would like to talk...

---

## 4. Modal Verbs ⭐⭐

I sometimes choose the wrong modal.

Practice differences between:

* can
* could
* may
* might
* must
* should
* would

Example:

❌ It can rain.
✅ It may/might rain.

---

## 5. Fixed Structures & Expressions ⭐⭐⭐

I need to learn common English structures as complete patterns rather than translating directly from Spanish.

Examples:

❌ I think Sarah is going to be happy... → sometimes I choose the wrong future form
❌ I would like talking...
✅ I would like to talk...

Practice common structures such as:

* be used to + -ing
* get used to + -ing
* look forward to + -ing
* would rather + past
* had better + base verb
* make + object + base verb
* let + object + base verb

---

## 6. Reported Speech ⭐⭐

I need more practice with tense changes and structures.

Examples:

❌ He asked me if I have finished.
✅ He asked me if I had finished.

❌ He told me that be quiet.
✅ He told me to be quiet.

Practice:

* said + that
* told + person + to
* asked + if/whether
* reported questions

---

## 7. Natural Vocabulary ⭐⭐

I sometimes know the general meaning but choose the wrong word.

Focus on learning **natural combinations and expressions**, not isolated vocabulary.

Examples from my mistakes:

* make progress
* take offence
* lose control
* be prepared to
* be set in the future
* get used to someone's way of life
* come in handy

---

## 8. Prepositions & Small Words ⭐

Pay attention to:

* for / since
* in / on / at
* to / for
* between / among
* articles
* some / any

These are small mistakes, but they affect accuracy.

---

# 🎯 Training Priority

Prioritize my mistakes in this order:

1. ⭐⭐⭐ Verb tenses
2. ⭐⭐⭐ Gerund vs infinitive
3. ⭐⭐⭐ Conditionals
4. ⭐⭐ Reported speech
5. ⭐⭐ Modal verbs
6. ⭐⭐ Fixed expressions
7. ⭐⭐ Natural vocabulary
8. ⭐ Prepositions and small words

Do not teach all of these at once.

During conversation, identify my mistakes naturally and repeatedly make me practice the structures I struggle with.

## Golden Rule

Don't just tell me the correct answer.

Make me **use the correct structure again in a new sentence**.
